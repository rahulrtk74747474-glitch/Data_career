# DataQuest — grounded seed for MiroFish product-viability simulation
Date: 2026-10-08 | Source: DataQuest repository feature/ecommerce-reproducible-workday-20261008
Purpose: simulate **possible narratives, objections and social reactions**, not predict conversion probabilities. All personas and scenario posts below are synthetic.

## Product reality: VERIFIED IN REPOSITORY
- DataQuest is an offline-first Flutter data analytics career simulation aimed at beginners and aspiring analysts, with training in SQL, spreadsheets, Python/Pandas concepts, statistics, Power BI concepts and business communication.
- Career progresses from Data Analyst Intern through Head of Analytics across simulated e-commerce, SaaS, banking, healthcare and logistics employers.
- First flagship e-commerce case has a versioned synthetic event dataset, SQLite cleaning view, real executed SQL assessment, a documented ₹16,350 net-revenue reference result, hints, draft saving and exportable project evidence. Remaining flagship projects are less rigorous and not all computation engines are real.
- SQL executes on embedded SQLite. Pandas and Excel use simulators. Power BI/DAX are not executed in Microsoft Power BI. Do not describe this as equivalent to certified production tool use.
- The app includes interview exercises, manager conversations, job-readiness scoring, local/offline progress, portfolio export and optional online continuity.
- Newest available development branch GitHub Actions run #414 completed successfully; Flutter static analysis, automated tests and Android builds passed. This supports **technical buildability**, not learning efficacy, crash-free use on real phones, job placement or market demand.
- First workday educational evidence is synthetic. Portfolio deliverables explicitly identify simulations; it does not constitute employer experience.
- No externally verified active-user, retention, price-conversion, learning-gain, placement or cohort statistics have been provided. Treat all such figures as UNKNOWN.

## Real context, NOT product evidence
Data analytics learners can choose free and paid alternatives including videos, textbooks, interactive coding products, portfolio websites and courses. Users may use mobile phones to learn but prefer desktop devices to write full SQL/Python and dashboards. These are product hypotheses, not measurements from our users.

## Social conversation model: what MiroFish actually simulates
MiroFish uses OASIS agents on Twitter/Reddit-style simulated social feeds. Treat those feeds as fictional conversations or stakeholder discussions, NOT real app sessions or actual interviews. Sentiment, likes, stated willingness-to-pay and viral reach are outcomes of the simulation setup, NOT measured users, market share, predicted revenue or outcomes.

## Conflict hooks (balanced — give agents reasons to DISAGREE)
- E-commerce SQL learner says the full reproducible data export is convincing; intermediate learner replies that Python/Power BI execution is still simulated.
- Android beginner likes offline capability but gets stuck on SQL syntax and fails the first workday; peer suggests hints; another says hints can make high scores less independent.
- Hiring manager challenges the claim "job-ready" and asks for unseen timed work and artifacts.
- Price-sensitive learner might pay only after a free, meaningful workday; founder argues revenue is needed to sustain the product.
- Instructor asks whether first workday correctly explains net revenue and refunds; reviewer points out one snapshot cannot prove revenue growth or causal impact.
- Accessibility tester reports friction typing complex SQL on mobile; founder proposes a desktop companion.
- Learners compare "career game" immersion with traditional tutorial sequences and debate whether the simulated manager seems realistic.
- Community moderator questions use of a name similar to an existing analytics education business and requests a distinct identity.

## Changes to debate as CONDITIONAL experiments, not facts
**A: Baseline early beta:** Offline mobile career game, simulated advanced tools, one reproducible SQL flagship, free introduction, no verified cohorts.
**B: Evidence-first:** Real SQL + complete projects and explicit limitations; unseen competency tasks, autosave/hints, transparent certificates, desktop companion later. No placement promises.
**C: Growth-first aggressive marketing:** Gamified badges, strong "job-ready" slogan, hypothetical paywall before full project, no new tool fidelity improvements.
Do not implicitly assume B is a success. Model negative, neutral and positive reactions across A/B/C.

## What to extract: mechanisms not magic conversion estimates
- Separate themes from each stakeholder group: first-lesson confusion, useful feedback, real execution, proof of skills, cost, device constraints, trust/branding, engagement, satisfaction.
- Highlight contradictory opinions and events that could change opinions (seeing a GitHub-replayable portfolio, trying to code on phone, failing a task, discovering a simulation limitation).
- Recommend **falsifiable, real-world user tests** for each dominant objection.
- Do NOT invent installation, D1/D7 retention, sales, conversion, review scores, hiring outcomes or statistical confidence.
- In final narrative distinguish (1) verified code/CI evidence, (2) MiroFish-generated synthetic discussion, (3) genuine user-study evidence which is currently absent.
